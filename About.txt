    <h2 class="section-title">How It Works</h2>
    <p class="section-sub">Three simple steps to recover a lost item.</p>

    <div class="info-grid mb-24">
      <div class="info-card">
        <div class="card-icon">&#128230;</div>
        <h3>1. Item Found &amp; Submitted</h3>
        <p>A student or staff member finds an item on campus and submits details through our online form. The physical item is handed in to Student Services or held by the finder pending collection.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="info-card">
        <div class="card-icon">&#128269;</div>
        <h3>2. Listed on the Portal</h3>
        <p>Within 24 hours, the item appears on the Home page listings table where all VUT community members can search and browse by name, category, or campus location.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="info-card">
        <div class="card-icon">&#10003;</div>
        <h3>3. Item Returned</h3>
        <p>The owner contacts Student Services or the finder directly via email to verify ownership and arrange collection. The listing is then updated to <em>Claimed</em>.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Purpose statement -->
    <div style="background:var(--white); border:1px solid var(--border); border-left:4px solid var(--blue); border-radius:var(--radius); padding:32px 36px; margin-bottom:40px; box-shadow:var(--shadow);">
      <h2 class="section-title">Purpose of This Portal</h2>
      <p style="color:var(--muted); line-height:1.8; margin-top:12px;">
        VUT is a large, active campus. Students and staff regularly misplace items such as phones, student cards, laptops, notebooks, and personal accessories.
        Previously, there was no central digital platform to report or search for found items — the community relied on notice boards and word of mouth.
        This portal digitises the lost-and-found process, making it accessible from any device, at any time.
      </p>
      <p style="color:var(--muted); line-height:1.8; margin-top:14px;">
        Developed as part of the <strong>Web Management 3.1</strong> module, Faculty of Applied and Computer Science, this project applies front-end web development
        principles to address a real problem experienced by the VUT student body.
      </p>
    </div>
